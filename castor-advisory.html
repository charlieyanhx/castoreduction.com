The Report

What the report covers.

Every Castor report includes the same ten sections, every time. No prompting required, no sections left out because you forgot to ask.

Viability Score
A 1 to 100 score with a confidence rating and plain-English interpretation of what the number means for your specific idea.
Executive Summary
The five findings that most affect your decision to move forward, written to be read in two minutes.
Competitive Landscape
Real direct and adjacent competitors, tagged and mapped. Filtered of irrelevant megabrands so you see the actual competitive field.
Market Sizing
TAM, SAM, and SOM estimates with sourcing and methodology shown so you can evaluate the numbers yourself.
Target Personas
1 to 3 buyer profiles built from real customer reviews across top competitors, with one identified as your best entry point.
Pricing Analysis
Price sensitivity modeling anchored to real competitor pricing data, not estimated ranges.
3-Year Revenue Scenarios
Conservative, base, and aggressive projections with the break-even year identified for each.
4Ps Marketing Plan
Product, price, place, and promotion with specific, actionable signals tied to your category and audience.
Critical Assumptions
The conditions that would have to hold for the viability score to be accurate, and what would invalidate it.
Numbered Citations
Every claim in the report links to a source. Nothing is fabricated to fill a section.
How It Works

Describe your business. Get your report.

1

Describe your business

Start a chat and describe your idea at whatever level you have it. A few sentences is enough to start. If the model needs more to generate an accurate report, it asks specific follow-up questions: what price point were you considering, where do you plan to sell, who is your target customer. You do not need to know the right questions to ask. The system does.

2

The pipeline runs

Castor pulls from real sources including competitor sites, customer review platforms, pricing pages, search trend data, and social signals. It runs them through a structured 14-step analysis pipeline and synthesizes the output into a single report. The process takes minutes, not days.

3

Download your report

Your report is ready in minutes. Download it as a PDF or receive it by email. No account required. Credits are tied to the email you use at purchase, so if you bought a pack, just enter the same email to run your next report.

Why Castor

What makes it different from just prompting an AI.

Consistent, structured output every time

When you prompt a general AI model, you get a different answer every time depending on how you ask. The output is reactive and unstructured. Castor runs a fixed pipeline that produces the same report format every time, regardless of how the input is phrased. You know what you're getting before you start.

It helps you articulate what you actually need

Most people don't know what questions to ask about their own business. The chat interface is designed to pull the right information from you through targeted follow-up questions. You don't need a research background or prior knowledge of market research frameworks to get a useful output.

Every claim has a source

Competitor prices come from real pricing pages. Personas are built from scraped customer reviews. Market sizing uses stated methodology and data quality indicators. Every section in the report cites where its data came from so you can verify it yourself.

Built for operators without research infrastructure

Large companies have research teams or budget to hire firms that charge thousands per engagement. Castor is priced and designed for the founder, the small business owner, and the lean investment firm that needs the same quality output without the overhead or the wait.
