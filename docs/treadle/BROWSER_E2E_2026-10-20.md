## Content findings requiring follow-up

1. **Revenue aggregation wording is wrong.** Summary and revenue findings say the
   streams cannot be aggregated. Raw tickets, subscribers, and licences cannot be added,
   but revenue can be combined after normalization to a common currency and period,
   with duplication and recognition rules handled. The prompt/review should enforce
   this distinction.
2. **Some assumptions become conclusions.** Shared staff is treated as a binding
   bottleneck despite unknown utilization; subscription capacity is ranked as least
   constrained without supporting operating data; institutional sales capability is
   assumed absent. These should remain conditional until supported.
3. **Workflow completion is not research completeness.** Market sizing is explicitly
   `partial`: it received three usable tool results but no final researcher answer.
   Customer voice correctly discloses that no direct customer evidence was found.
   Revenue supplies conditional frameworks, not numeric forecasts.
4. **Review sensitivity needs improvement.** The consistency reviewer returned no issues
   despite the semantic problems above. This live run therefore does not establish that
   correction loops detect those problems. Their two-round bound remains covered by
   offline workflow tests.
5. **PREP has little timing headroom.** This sample finished about 19 seconds before its
   task deadline. Measure latency on more complex inputs before choosing production
   limits; this run alone is not a reliability benchmark.

External factual claims and competitor applicability were not independently verified.
Source tracing remains disabled as requested. The receipt's zero-dollar estimate should
not be treated as a free run: pricing for the selected model aliases is not represented
in the current estimator.
