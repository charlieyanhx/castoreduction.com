# Castor Advisories — Content Reference

## Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Blend of startup energy + institutional rigor
- **Style**: Confident, precise, grounded. Never hype. Never corporate-bland.
- **Influences**: Stripe's clarity, Linear's directness, Bridgewater's intellectual honesty
- **Rules**:
  - Lead with what's different, not what's good
  - Show the system, not just the outcome
  - Respect the reader's intelligence — no hand-holding
  - Use active voice. Cut filler. Every sentence should earn its place.
  - Avoid: "leverage", "synergy", "disruption", "cutting-edge", "world-class"

## Key Facts
- **What**: A closed-loop accelerator connecting graduates with real consulting engagements
- **Revenue**: Consulting, ventures (equity participation), talent intelligence, market data
- **Compensation model**: Equity/revenue share — participants earn ownership in ventures they help build
- **Recruiter fees**: Success-based, intentionally vague in public materials — designed as conversation starter
- **Target clients**: SMBs, 10–200 employees, operationally-focused
- **Differentiator**: Every engagement feeds back into the system — consulting informs products, products improve delivery, talent signal creates recruiting value
