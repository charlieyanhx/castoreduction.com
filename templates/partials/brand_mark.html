{# THE BRAND MARK, ONCE. It was hand-copied into seven pages: five app pages carrying an identical 1,364-character SVG, and two password pages that carried something else entirely: U+1FAAB LOW BATTERY, served as the company logo, inside a with no CSS behind it. Nobody introduced that on purpose; it is what copying markup by hand eventually produces, and nothing could have caught it because there was no definition to disagree with. `home` is the only thing that legitimately varies: the survey sends a visitor to the marketing page, every signed-in surface sends them to /home. #}
