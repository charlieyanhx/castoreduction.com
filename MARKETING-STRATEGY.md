# Castor Advisories: Go-to-Market Strategy

**Stage**: Pre-launch
**Budget**: $1-5K/month
**Primary audiences**: Participants (graduates) + Clients (SMBs), simultaneously
**Secondary audiences**: Recruiters, Investors

---

## 1. The Core Challenge

Castor has a cold-start problem: you need talent to serve clients, and you need clients to attract talent. Neither audience will commit without evidence the other side exists. Every early action must generate signal for both sides simultaneously.

**Principle**: Every piece of content, every outreach, every conversation should be legible to at least two audiences. A LinkedIn post about a client engagement also signals to graduates that the work is real. A participant spotlight also signals to clients that the talent is serious.

---

## 2. Positioning & Messaging

### Master Positioning
> Castor is a structured accelerator where high-potential graduates earn equity by solving real business problems, generating proprietary insight, validated talent signal, and compounding value for everyone in the system.

### Audience-Specific Messaging

#### Participants (Graduates)
**Core message**: "Real work. Real equity. A track record that speaks for itself."

| Pain point | Castor's answer |
|---|---|
| Entry-level jobs are credential-gated | Performance-validated profiles that bypass resume filters |
| Internships are busywork | Supervised consulting on live business challenges |
| No ownership or upside | Equity in the ventures you help build |
| No proof of capability | Documented performance record shared with recruiting partners |

**Tone**: Direct, ambitious, peer-to-peer. Don't talk down. Don't sound like a bootcamp. Sound like a serious opportunity for serious people.

**Key phrases**:
- "Earn equity, not certificates"
- "Your work is your introduction"
- "Built for people who want to build"
- "Track record > transcript"

#### Clients (SMBs)
**Core message**: "Consulting that delivers implementations, not slide decks. At a price point that makes sense."

| Pain point | Castor's answer |
|---|---|
| Big firms are expensive and slow | Outcome-aligned fees, not hourly billing |
| Consultants hand you a report and leave | Supervised teams that implement and transfer ownership |
| Hard to find help for operational problems | Teams embedded in your workflows, systems, and constraints |
| Skeptical of junior talent | Structured supervision + documented methodology |

**Tone**: Pragmatic, credible, no-nonsense. Speak to operators, not executives playing strategy theater. Acknowledge that hiring a team of graduates requires trust, and explain exactly how supervision and methodology earn it.

**Key phrases**:
- "Implementation over advisory"
- "Fees tied to outcomes, not hours"
- "Supervised teams, real delivery"
- "Built for companies that value execution"

#### Recruiters (Secondary)
**Core message**: "Better information. Better candidates. Shared success."

- Activate after the first cohort has performance data to share
- Position as a value-add to their existing pipeline, not a replacement
- Lead with the data: "Here's what a Castor profile looks like vs. a standard resume"

#### Investors (Secondary)
**Core message**: "A system designed to compound value at every stage."

- Activate selectively. Inbound-driven, not outbound.
- The website's investor page is the primary tool
- Share materials only after a qualifying conversation

## 3. Channel Strategy

### Budget allocation ($3K/month recommended starting point)

| Channel | Monthly spend | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (organic + light promotion) | $500 | Primary content distribution |
| Email tooling (Apollo, Instantly, or similar) | $200 | Cold outreach to clients + universities |
| Domain & email infrastructure | $50 | Professional email, warm-up |
| Design tools (Canva Pro or Figma) | $15 | Social content, one-pagers |
| Landing page / analytics | $0 | Already built (the website) |
| Reserve / testing | $235-$2,235 | A/B testing, sponsored posts, events |
| **Total** | **$1,000-$3,000** | |

### Channel breakdown by audience

#### Participants: Where to reach them

1. **LinkedIn** (primary)
   - Graduates actively use LinkedIn for career content
   - Post 3-4x/week from the founder's personal account (not the company page; personal gets 10x reach)
   - Content themes: what real consulting looks like, what equity means for early-career, why credentials are broken, behind-the-scenes of engagements

2. **University channels** (high-leverage, free)
   - Career services offices: request to post on job boards or present at career fairs
   - Student entrepreneurship clubs and consulting clubs
   - Professor referrals (especially in business, CS, engineering)
   - Target 10-15 universities in your geographic area
   - **Template email** (see Outreach Playbooks below)

3. **Reddit / Hacker News / Twitter/X** (organic, selective)
   - r/careerguidance, r/consulting, r/startups. Provide genuine value, don't spam.
   - HN: "Show HN" when you have a product to show; "Ask HN" style posts about the graduate hiring problem
   - Twitter/X: Only if the founder is already active there. Don't start from zero.

4. **Referrals**
   - Every accepted participant should be asked: "Who else should know about this?"
   - Offer early access or priority review for referred candidates

#### Clients: Where to reach them

1. **Cold outreach** (primary)
   - Target: Operations managers, founders, and COOs at companies with 10-200 employees
   - Industries: logistics, professional services, e-commerce, healthcare admin, construction. Any operationally-heavy SMB.
   - Volume: 50-100 personalized emails/week
   - Tool: Apollo.io or Instantly.ai for sequences
   - **Template sequences** (see Outreach Playbooks below)

2. **LinkedIn** (founder's account)
   - Same content strategy as for participants: posts about operational problems, case studies (even hypothetical pre-launch), how AI is changing SMB operations
   - Direct outreach to prospects who engage with your content

3. **Local business networks**
   - Chamber of Commerce events
   - Small business accelerators and incubators (many are looking for exactly this kind of support for their cohort companies)
   - BNI or similar referral groups (low cost, high trust)

4. **Warm introductions**
   - Ask everyone in your network: "Who do you know running a company with 20-100 people that's drowning in manual processes?"
   - This single question, asked to 50 people, will generate your first 3-5 clients.

## 4. Cold Outreach Playbooks

### Playbook A: University Career Services

**Subject**: Structured consulting opportunity for graduates, [University Name]

**Email**:
> Hi [Name],
>
> I run Castor Advisories, a structured accelerator that places graduates into supervised consulting engagements with real business clients.
>
> Participants work on live operational challenges, build real solutions, and earn equity in the ventures they help create. Every engagement produces a documented performance profile that our recruiting partners use for placement.
>
> We're currently building our next cohort and I'd love to share this with students at [University]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if it's a fit for your career board or an upcoming event?
>
> Best,
> [Name]

**Follow-up (Day 3)**: Short, adds a specific data point or differentiator.
**Follow-up (Day 7)**: Offer to send a one-pager they can share directly.

---

### Playbook B: Client Cold Outreach (SMB Operations)

**Sequence: 3 emails over 10 days**

**Email 1: The hook**
Subject: Quick question about [specific operational area]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I noticed [Company] is growing quickly in [area]. Congratulations. Quick question: are your internal operations keeping up with that growth, or are there workflows that still run on spreadsheets and manual handoffs?
>
> We run supervised consulting teams that help companies like yours fix exactly that. Process automation, data consolidation, workflow redesign. Fees are tied to outcomes, not hours. And we typically work with companies your size (10-200 employees) where the ROI on operational improvements is immediate.
>
> Worth a 15-minute conversation?
>
> [Name], Castor Advisories

**Email 2 (Day 4): Social proof / specificity**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up briefly. To give you a concrete sense of what we do: a typical engagement might involve mapping your [order fulfillment / client onboarding / data reporting] process, identifying the manual steps that are costing you time, and building an implemented solution. Not a report.
>
> Our teams are supervised and our methodology is documented. Happy to walk through how it works if you're curious.

**Email 3 (Day 10): The breakup**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Keeping this short. I know you're busy. If operational efficiency isn't a priority right now, no worries at all. But if it is, I'd genuinely enjoy the conversation.
>
> Either way, I'll stop filling your inbox. Have a great week.

---

### Playbook C: Graduate Direct Outreach

**Where**: LinkedIn DMs to graduates who post about job searching, career frustration, or entrepreneurship.

**Message**:
> Hey [Name], saw your post about [topic]. Resonated with me.
>
> I run Castor Advisories. We place graduates into supervised consulting teams that work on real business challenges. You earn equity in what you build and leave with a documented performance record that recruiting partners actually use.
>
> Not a bootcamp, not an internship. Real work with real stakes. Would you be up for a quick chat to see if it's a fit?

**Key rule**: Only reach out to people who have posted something relevant. Never spray and pray.

## 5. Content Strategy

### The Founder's LinkedIn (primary content engine)

Post 3-4x per week. Mix of these formats:

| Format | Frequency | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot take** | 1x/week | "Unpaid internships are a market failure, not a rite of passage." |
| **System thinking** | 1x/week | "Why we structured Castor as a closed loop, and what that means for everyone in the system." |
| **Behind the scenes** | 1x/week | "Here's what our first client engagement actually looks like, week by week." |
| **Signal boost** | 1x/week | Share a participant's work, a client win, or an industry insight with your commentary |

### Content pillars (themes to rotate through)

1. **The hiring problem**: Why credentials don't predict performance, why early-career hiring is broken, what better looks like
2. **Operations at SMBs**: The pain of manual processes, what automation actually looks like (not AI hype), real operational improvements
3. **The Castor model**: How the flywheel works, why equity for graduates matters, what "outcome-aligned consulting" means in practice
4. **Builder culture**: Profiles of the kind of people Castor is for, what ambition looks like without privilege, the case for learning by doing

### Newsletter (start in month 2)

- Biweekly, short (500 words max)
- Mix of: 1 insight about the graduate/SMB market + 1 update on Castor's progress
- Use Buttondown or Substack (free tier)
- Collect emails from the website contact forms. Add a checkbox: "Keep me updated on Castor"
- This becomes your investor update channel too (BCC investors on the same newsletter)

## 6. Recruiter Activation (Month 3+)

Don't approach recruiters until you have real performance data from at least one completed cohort.

**When ready**:
1. Build a sample anonymized performance profile. Show what the data looks like.
2. Identify 20-30 recruiting agencies that specialize in early-career placement in your geography
3. Cold email with the sample profile as the hook:
   > "Here's what a Castor talent profile looks like. It captures 6 performance dimensions from 12 weeks of observed work. We're looking for recruiting partners who want access to this signal. Fees are success-based. You only pay when a placement works."
4. Offer the first 2-3 placements at a reduced rate or free to build trust

---

## 7. Investor Activation (Opportunistic)

Don't run an investor outreach campaign. Instead:

1. **Let the website do the work.** The investor page is already strong.
2. **Share the newsletter.** Forward it to 5-10 target investors monthly with a short personal note.
3. **Warm intros only.** Ask advisors, mentors, and early supporters to make introductions.
4. **Milestone triggers.** Send a brief update when you hit a meaningful milestone:
   - First client signed
   - First cohort assembled
   - First engagement delivered
   - First recruiting placement made
   - First venture spun out

**Template for milestone outreach**:
> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick update on Castor. We just [milestone]. This means [what it validates about the model].
>
> Our full operating model and financials are available if you'd like a deeper look. Happy to make time for a conversation.

## 8. 90-Day Launch Sequence

### Week 1-2: Foundation
- [ ] Set up LinkedIn profile optimized for Castor (founder account)
- [ ] Set up professional email (contact@castoradvisories.com) with warm-up
- [ ] Set up Apollo.io or Instantly.ai for outreach sequences
- [ ] Create a one-pager PDF for participants (share with universities)
- [ ] Create a one-pager PDF for clients (attach to cold emails)
- [ ] Identify 15 target universities and find career services contacts
- [ ] Identify 100 target SMBs in your geography (use Apollo for prospecting)

### Week 3-4: First Outreach Wave
- [ ] Send university outreach emails (Playbook A), 15 universities
- [ ] Launch client cold outreach (Playbook B), 50 emails/week
- [ ] Start posting on LinkedIn, 3x/week minimum
- [ ] Begin direct LinkedIn outreach to graduates (Playbook C), 10-15/week
- [ ] Ask 50 people in your network the warm intro question for clients
- [ ] **Target**: 3-5 client conversations, 10-15 participant applications

### Week 5-8: Build Momentum
- [ ] Continue cold outreach cadence (50 client emails/week, 15 grad DMs/week)
- [ ] Follow up with university contacts. Offer to do a virtual info session.
- [ ] Increase LinkedIn posting to 4x/week
- [ ] Launch 1-2 promoted LinkedIn posts ($200-500) targeting SMB decision-makers in your area
- [ ] Collect and respond to inbound from the website
- [ ] Start refining messaging based on what's resonating (track reply rates)
- [ ] **Target**: First client signed, 20-30 participant applications in pipeline

### Week 9-12: Close and Launch
- [ ] Close first 2-3 client engagements
- [ ] Select first cohort of participants (aim for 8-15)
- [ ] Announce the cohort launch on LinkedIn (this is your biggest content moment)
- [ ] Set up the biweekly newsletter, send the first edition
- [ ] Begin documenting the first engagement for future content
- [ ] Send milestone update to 5-10 target investors
- [ ] **Target**: Program launched, 2-3 active engagements, newsletter live

## 9. Metrics & KPIs

### Leading indicators (track weekly)

| Metric | Target (Month 1) | Target (Month 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Cold emails sent (clients) | 200/month | 200/month |
| Client reply rate | 8-12% | 12-18% |
| Client conversations booked | 5-8 | 10-15 |
| Participant applications | 15-20 | 40-60 |
| LinkedIn post impressions | 2K-5K/week | 10K-20K/week |
| Website unique visitors | 200/month | 500-1K/month |

### Lagging indicators (track monthly)

| Metric | Target (Month 3) |
|---|---|
| Clients signed | 2-3 |
| Participants accepted into cohort | 8-15 |
| Newsletter subscribers | 100-200 |
| Recruiting partner conversations | 2-3 |
| Investor conversations (inbound) | 1-2 |

### What to optimize

- **If client reply rates are low (<5%)**: Rewrite subject lines, test different operational pain points, try different company sizes
- **If participant applications are low**: Test different channels (Reddit, Twitter), try university info sessions, ask current applicants where they heard about you
- **If LinkedIn isn't growing**: Post more hot takes (controversy drives reach), comment actively on other people's posts, test carousel/document formats
- **If website traffic is low but outreach is working**: That's fine. Direct outreach converts better than inbound at this stage. Don't over-index on SEO yet.

## 10. What NOT to Do

1. **Don't build a blog yet.** LinkedIn is your blog. A blog on a startup website with no domain authority is a graveyard for good writing.
2. **Don't run paid ads to cold audiences.** Your budget is too small for awareness campaigns. Use paid only to amplify content that's already working organically.
3. **Don't try to be on every platform.** LinkedIn is your primary channel. Everything else is supplementary.
4. **Don't hire a marketing person yet.** The founder's voice is the most authentic and effective channel at this stage. Delegate outreach execution (sequences, data entry), not strategy or content.
5. **Don't pitch investors before you have a milestone.** A pre-revenue deck is worth 10x less than a "we just signed our third client and here's why" email.
6. **Don't wait for everything to be perfect.** The website is ready. The messaging is ready. The only thing missing is motion.
